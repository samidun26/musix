      Getting around

      • On the piano, the knobs and the beat grid, Tab moves between areas and arrow keys move inside them.
      • Knobs: arrows nudge, Page Up/Page Down make big jumps, Home/End go to the ends. Hold Shift while dragging for fine control.
      • Hard to hold keys down? Turn on Hold: tap once to start a note, tap again to stop it.
      • Not sure what to play? Set Scale lock to “Major pentatonic” — every key sounds good.
